# Pragmatic Framework skills (2027)

These skills put the **2027 Pragmatic Framework** from Pragmatic Institute into practice: 37 focus areas in 7 categories, from strategy on the left to execution on the right. There is one skill per focus area, plus `pragmatic-framework-overview`, which explains the Framework, routes requests to the right skill and runs a team assessment.

Every skill is named `pragmatic-<focus-area>`, and its folder name matches the `name` in its frontmatter.

| Category | Question | Focus area → skill |
|---|---|---|
| **Market** | What problems does the market have, and where can we win? | [Market Problems](pragmatic-market-problems/) · [Product Strategy](pragmatic-product-strategy/) · [Distinctive Competencies](pragmatic-distinctive-competencies/) · [Win/Loss Analysis](pragmatic-win-loss-analysis/) · [Competitive Landscape](pragmatic-competitive-landscape/) |
| **Focus** | Where should we play, and where should we invest? | [Market Definition](pragmatic-market-definition/) · [Distribution Strategy](pragmatic-distribution-strategy/) · [Product Portfolio](pragmatic-product-portfolio/) · [Product Roadmap](pragmatic-product-roadmap/) |
| **Business** | Is this a good investment, and is it paying off? | [Business Plan](pragmatic-business-plan/) · [Pricing](pragmatic-pricing/) · [Buy, Build or Partner](pragmatic-buy-build-partner/) · [Business Outcomes](pragmatic-business-outcomes/) · [Measurement](pragmatic-measurement/) |
| **Planning** | What are we building, for whom, and why? | [Positioning](pragmatic-positioning/) · [Buyer Journey](pragmatic-buyer-journey/) · [Buyer Personas](pragmatic-buyer-personas/) · [User Personas](pragmatic-user-personas/) · [Requirements](pragmatic-requirements/) · [Ideation](pragmatic-ideation/) · [Stakeholder Management](pragmatic-stakeholder-management/) |
| **Go-to-Market** | How will the market find, choose and stay with the product? | [Marketing Plan](pragmatic-marketing-plan/) · [Growth](pragmatic-growth/) · [Retention](pragmatic-retention/) · [Launch](pragmatic-launch/) · [Organizational Alignment](pragmatic-organizational-alignment/) · [Awareness](pragmatic-awareness/) · [Engagement](pragmatic-engagement/) · [Customer Satisfaction](pragmatic-customer-satisfaction/) |
| **Enablement** | Can the people around the product make it succeed? | [Adoption](pragmatic-adoption/) · [Content](pragmatic-content/) · [Assets](pragmatic-assets/) · [Channel Support](pragmatic-channel-support/) |
| **Operations** | Is the product delivering value as it grows? | [Scaling](pragmatic-scaling/) · [Exceptions](pragmatic-exceptions/) · [Escalations](pragmatic-escalations/) · [Customer Value Realization](pragmatic-customer-value-realization/) |

The [quick reference](pragmatic-framework-overview/references/framework-2027.md) lists every focus-area question, whether it sits above or below the bar, and the boundaries between neighboring focus areas.

## AI

The 2027 Framework has no AI box: AI supports work across every focus area. Each skill ends with a short **Using AI in this focus area** section. The rules are the same in every skill: verify AI output against market evidence, protect sensitive information, and keep people accountable for decisions.
