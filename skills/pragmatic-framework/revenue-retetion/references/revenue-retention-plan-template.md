# Revenue Retention Plan Template

Use this template to structure a comprehensive Revenue Retention plan. Adapt sections based on the user's business model, maturity, and available data. Not every section will apply to every organization.

---

## Recommended Plan Structure

### 1. Executive Summary

A concise overview (1 page max) covering:
- Current state of revenue retention (headline metrics)
- Key opportunities and risks identified
- Strategic priorities for the planning period
- Expected outcomes and investment required

### 2. Customer Base Analysis

#### 2.1 Revenue Composition
- Total revenue from existing customers vs. new customers
- Revenue by customer segment (size, industry, product, geography)
- Recurring vs. non-recurring revenue breakdown
- Contract type distribution (annual, multi-year, month-to-month)

#### 2.2 Retention Metrics Baseline
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR) — current, trailing 12 months, trend
- Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) — current, trailing 12 months, trend
- Customer retention rate (logo retention)
- Revenue churn rate (voluntary + involuntary breakdown)
- Expansion revenue rate
- Customer Lifetime Value by segment

#### 2.3 Churn Analysis
- Top churn reasons (from exit surveys, Win/Loss interviews, support data)
- Churn by segment — which segments have highest/lowest churn?
- Churn timeline — when in the customer lifecycle does churn concentrate?
- Involuntary churn rate and causes
- Competitive losses vs. other churn drivers

#### 2.4 Customer Health Overview
- Health score distribution (if available)
- Product adoption and engagement trends
- NPS/CSAT trends by segment
- Support ticket trends and sentiment

### 3. Strategic Priorities

Based on the analysis, define 3-5 strategic priorities for the planning period. For each priority, include:
- Rationale (what data supports this priority)
- Expected impact on NRR/GRR
- Owner and key stakeholders
- Timeline

Example priorities:
- Reduce voluntary churn in mid-market segment from 12% to 8%
- Increase expansion revenue from enterprise accounts by 20%
- Eliminate involuntary churn through payment recovery automation
- Launch customer health scoring to enable proactive intervention
- Build customer advisory board for top 20 accounts

### 4. Retention Programs

For each program or initiative, document:

#### Program Name
- **Objective:** What does this program aim to achieve?
- **Target segment:** Which customers does this serve?
- **Strategy pillar:** Protect (reduce churn), Grow (expansion), or Deepen (loyalty)
- **Key activities:** What specific actions will be taken?
- **Timeline:** When does it start? Key milestones?
- **Owner:** Who is accountable?
- **Dependencies:** What must be in place for this to work?
- **Budget:** What resources and funding are needed?
- **Success metrics:** How will impact be measured?

#### Common Program Types

**Churn Reduction Programs:**
- At-risk customer intervention workflow
- Renewal process optimization
- Involuntary churn recovery (dunning, payment retry)
- Customer health monitoring and alerting
- Win-back campaigns for recently churned customers
- Onboarding optimization to reduce early-lifecycle churn

**Expansion Revenue Programs:**
- Upsell campaign by segment/trigger
- Cross-sell program for complementary products
- Usage-based expansion outreach
- Annual price increase strategy
- Customer success-driven expansion (CSQLs)
- Product-led expansion (in-app prompts, usage gates)

**Loyalty and Engagement Programs:**
- Customer loyalty/rewards program
- Customer advisory board (CAB)
- Customer education and certification programs
- User community and peer networking
- Executive sponsor program for strategic accounts
- Customer appreciation events
- Personalized communication cadences
- Voice-of-customer feedback loops

#### 6.2 Leading Indicators
Metrics that predict future retention outcomes:

- Product engagement/adoption rates

#### 6.3 Reporting Cadence
- Weekly: Key operational metrics (churn alerts, at-risk accounts, expansion pipeline)
- Monthly: Full retention dashboard review with team
- Quarterly: Executive review of NRR/GRR trends, program performance, budget utilization
- Annually: Comprehensive retention strategy review and next-year planning

#### 6.4 Strategy Adjustment Triggers
Define specific thresholds that trigger strategy reassessment:
- Churn rate exceeds X% in any month
- NRR drops below X% for two consecutive quarters
- A top-10 account signals intent to leave
- A specific segment's health scores decline by more than X points

### 7. Cross-Functional Dependencies

Revenue Retention cannot succeed in isolation. Document dependencies on:

- **Product team** — Feature requests from retention analysis, roadmap alignment with customer needs
- **Sales** — Expansion selling motions, renewal process, account management
- **Customer Success** — Health monitoring, QBRs, onboarding optimization
- **Support** — Escalation processes, resolution quality, feedback routing
- **Finance** — Pricing flexibility for saves, budget approval, revenue recognition
- **Marketing (other programs)** — Content for lifecycle campaigns, advocacy program coordination

## Adaptation Notes

- **Early-stage companies** may not have enough data for a full plan. Focus on establishing baseline metrics, building the measurement infrastructure, and implementing 2-3 high-impact retention programs.
- **Enterprise-heavy businesses** should weight the plan toward named-account strategies, executive engagement, and customer success-driven expansion.
- **Self-serve / PLG businesses** should weight toward product-led retention (onboarding optimization, in-app engagement, automated lifecycle campaigns) and usage-based expansion triggers.
- **Non-SaaS businesses** should adapt the recurring-revenue metrics to their contract/purchasing model. The strategic pillars (Protect, Grow, Deepen) apply universally.
