**Briefing Tips**:
- Lead with status — don't bury risks at the end
- Frame everything in terms executives care about: revenue, market share, customer impact, risk
- Request decisions explicitly — don't hope executives will infer what you need
- Keep it to 1–2 pages; link to detail rather than embedding it
