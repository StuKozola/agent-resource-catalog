## Instructions

Complete one row per stakeholder audience. For each audience, define:
- **Purpose**: What decision or action should this communication enable?
- **Key Content**: What information does this audience need?
- **Format**: How will you deliver it (doc, email, meeting, Slack, etc.)?
- **Frequency**: How often, and when in the product cycle?
- **Owner**: Who is responsible for preparing and delivering this communication?
- **Feedback Loop**: How will you know this communication is working?

## Stakeholder Communication Plan

| Audience | Purpose | Key Content | Format | Frequency | Owner | Feedback Loop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Executives** | Strategic alignment; surface risks and decisions needed | Progress vs. goals, business impact, risks, decisions needed | Executive briefing doc | Monthly | PM | Response to briefing; questions in exec meeting |
| **Engineering** | Market context for priorities; advance notice of changes | Market rationale for top priorities; scope or priority changes | Sprint context notes; in-person for major changes | Per sprint + triggered by changes | PM | Questions during sprint planning; developers citing market context |
| **Sales** | Release readiness; accurate customer conversation guidance | Upcoming features, target personas, competitive implications, talking points | Email update + optional Q&A | Bi-weekly OR 4–6 weeks before each release | PMM | Sales questions; win/loss patterns related to roadmap |
| **Product Marketing** | Launch planning and messaging | Upcoming release themes, target personas, market problems solved, timing | PM–PMM sync + launch briefing | Monthly sync + 6–8 wks before major release | PM + PMM | Campaign alignment; messaging accuracy |
| **Customer Success** | Customer impact awareness | What's changing, who's affected, what to tell customers | Internal release notes + briefing | 2–4 weeks before each release | PM | CS questions; customer escalations related to changes |
| **Key Customers** | Strategic alignment + feedback | Roadmap themes, problems being addressed, invitation to input | Roadmap dialogue (not a presentation) | Quarterly | PM | Customer input incorporated; relationship health |
| **Channel Partners** | Enablement and integration planning | Roadmap direction, release timing, integration changes | Partner update + enablement guide | Quarterly + per major release | PM + PMM | Partner enablement success; integration issues |

## Out-of-Cycle Communication Triggers

Define which events require unplanned communications — and to whom:

| Trigger Event | Audiences to Notify | Timing | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Major scope reduction or feature cut | Executives, Sales, CS | Immediately | Email + meeting |
| Significant priority reprioritization | Engineering, PMM, Sales | Before next sprint | Email + meeting |
| Missed milestone | Executives | As soon as known | Briefing update |
| Significant competitive development | Executives, Sales, PMM | Within 48 hours | Email |
| New market insight that changes direction | Executives, Engineering | Per planning cycle | Briefing + meeting |
| Customer-impacting issue in release | CS, Sales | Before release | Internal release note |
