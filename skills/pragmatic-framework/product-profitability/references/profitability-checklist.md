# Product Profitability Review Checklist

Use this checklist when conducting a product profitability review. It works for quarterly deep-dives or annual audits. Adjust scope based on the review cadence.

---

## Pre-Review Preparation

- [ ] Gather the latest product P&L (or build one if it doesn't exist)
- [ ] Collect KPI data for the review period and at least 2 prior periods for trend comparison
- [ ] Identify the product's lifecycle stage (pre-launch, growth, mature, declining)
- [ ] Confirm cost allocation methodology hasn't changed since last review (if it has, restate prior periods)
- [ ] Pull benchmark data for the product's industry and stage

## Revenue Analysis

- [ ] Is total product revenue growing, flat, or declining compared to prior period?
- [ ] What is driving the trend? (volume, pricing, mix, new segments, churn)
- [ ] Is revenue concentrated in a few customers/segments? What is the risk?
- [ ] Are there seasonal patterns that explain current performance?
- [ ] For subscription products: What is Net Revenue Retention? Above or below 100%?
- [ ] Are discounts, refunds, or credits trending upward? (These erode net revenue silently)

## Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) Analysis

- [ ] Has COGS per unit / per customer changed? Up or down? Why?
- [ ] Are there COGS components that have increased disproportionately?
- [ ] For SaaS: Review hosting/infrastructure costs relative to usage growth — are they scaling linearly or are there economies of scale?
- [ ] For physical products: Review supplier pricing, manufacturing costs, shipping rates
- [ ] Is there waste, rework, or inefficiency in delivery that inflates COGS?

## Gross Margin Assessment

- [ ] What is the current gross margin %? Compare to prior periods.
- [ ] Is gross margin expanding, stable, or compressing?
- [ ] How does it compare to industry benchmarks for this product type and stage?
- [ ] If compressing: Is it a revenue problem (pricing pressure) or a cost problem (COGS rising)?
- [ ] Are there specific product lines, SKUs, or customer segments with significantly different margins?

## Variable Cost & Contribution Margin Assessment

- [ ] What are the variable costs beyond COGS? (commissions, transaction fees, variable marketing)
- [ ] What is the contribution margin %?
- [ ] Is contribution margin positive? If not, the product loses money on every sale — this is urgent.
- [ ] For products with high contribution margin but low operating margin: Is the team overhead justified by growth or strategic value?

## Operating Expense Review

- [ ] What is the team size allocated to this product? Has it changed?
- [ ] What is the R&D investment (capitalized and expensed) as % of revenue?
- [ ] What is the sales & marketing spend allocated to this product?
- [ ] Are G&A allocations reasonable and consistent with prior periods?
- [ ] Is operating margin positive? If not, at what revenue level would it break even?

## Customer Economics (if applicable)

- [ ] What is the current CAC? Trending up or down?
- [ ] What is the LTV:CAC ratio? Above 3:1?
- [ ] What is the CAC payback period? Under 12-18 months?
- [ ] Is churn rate stable, improving, or worsening?
- [ ] Are there customer cohorts with significantly different economics?

## Operational Impact

- [ ] What support burden does this product create? (tickets per customer, support cost per customer)
- [ ] Does this product create infrastructure or platform complexity that affects other products?
- [ ] What is the opportunity cost of the team working on this product versus alternatives?
- [ ] Are there technical debt or reliability issues driving hidden costs?

## Strategic Assessment

- [ ] Is this product's profitability trajectory aligned with the original Business Plan projections?
- [ ] Should pricing be adjusted based on current margin analysis?
- [ ] Is the product earning its place in the portfolio? (Does it justify the resources it consumes?)
- [ ] Are there clear actions to improve profitability? (cost reduction, pricing change, growth investment, feature pruning, customer segment focus)
- [ ] Should this product be grown, maintained, restructured, or sunset?

## Post-Review Actions

- [ ] Document findings in a profitability analysis report
- [ ] Update the product P&L with any corrected allocations
- [ ] Communicate results to stakeholders (use Stakeholder Communications cadence)
- [ ] Feed findings into Business Plan review and Pricing review processes
- [ ] Set specific targets and action items for the next review period
- [ ] Schedule the next review
