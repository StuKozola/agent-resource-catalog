# Channel Information Needs Framework

Channel partners need three categories of information to effectively pursue and close opportunities. The Channels activity ensures this information flows consistently, is current, and is tailored to partner needs.

## Category 1: Market Intelligence

Market intelligence helps channel partners understand the landscape they're selling into. This is information about the market — not about your product.

### What to Provide

**Industry and Segment Context**
- Market trends affecting target segments (regulatory changes, technology shifts, economic factors)
- Segment-specific challenges and priorities that create urgency for your solution category
- Market sizing data relevant to the partner's territory or vertical focus

**Buyer Behavior Insights**
- How buyers in target segments evaluate and purchase solutions
- Common triggers that initiate a buying cycle
- Decision-making dynamics — who influences, who decides, who signs
- Typical sales cycle length and stages by segment

**Competitive Intelligence**
- Competitive landscape updates — new entrants, acquisitions, product launches
- Competitive positioning guidance — where you win, where you're vulnerable
- Competitive displacement strategies with supporting talk tracks
- Status quo and "do nothing" as competitors — how to create urgency against inertia

### Cadence and Format
- **Quarterly:** Market trend summaries, competitive landscape updates
- **As-needed:** Competitive alerts when significant competitor moves occur
- **Annually:** Market sizing and segment prioritization refresh

## Category 2: Solution Information

Solution information helps channel partners understand what they're selling and how it solves buyer problems. This bridges product knowledge (taught in Channel Training) with ongoing updates.

### What to Provide

**Product and Solution Updates**
- New feature releases and their business impact (not just technical specifications)
- Problem-to-feature mapping — which market problems each capability addresses
- Integration and compatibility updates (especially for SI and VAR partners)
- Roadmap highlights — what's coming that partners can use to create forward-looking conversations

**Technical Guidance**
- Solution architecture and deployment models
- Sizing and configuration guidance for common scenarios
- Technical prerequisites and compatibility matrices
- Known limitations and workarounds (partners lose credibility when surprises emerge)

**Pricing and Packaging**
- Current pricing schedules and discount guidelines
- Packaging options and how to match them to customer needs
- Margin structures and partner economics
- Promotional pricing or special offers with clear terms and timelines

**Use Cases and Success Stories**
- Documented use cases by segment and persona
- Customer success stories and case studies (with permission for partner use)
- ROI data and value quantification frameworks
- Reference customer availability for partner-led deals

### Cadence and Format
- **Per release:** Product update briefs tied to each release or major update
- **Monthly:** Solution digest summarizing new collateral, case studies, and pricing updates
- **Real-time:** Pricing and promotion updates effective immediately upon change

## Category 3: Opportunity Support

Opportunity support is deal-specific assistance that helps partners win individual opportunities. This is the most resource-intensive category but often the most impactful.

### What to Provide

**Deal Support**
- Custom competitive positioning for specific deal situations
- Tailored use case development for prospect-specific scenarios
- ROI and business case assistance using prospect-specific data
- Executive engagement — arranging vendor executive involvement in strategic deals
- Technical pre-sales support — solution architects or engineers for complex evaluations

**Proof Points and Validation**
- Reference customer introductions matched to prospect industry and use case
- Proof-of-concept or trial support for partner-led evaluations
- Analyst reports and third-party validation materials
- Security, compliance, and procurement documentation

**Co-Selling Resources**
- Joint call or meeting support with vendor subject matter experts
- Proposal and RFP assistance
- Demo environment access and customization support
- Post-sale implementation planning support for complex deals

### Cadence and Format
- **On-demand:** Triggered by partner request through defined escalation process
- **SLA-based:** Define response time expectations by request type and partner tier

## Information Needs by Channel Type

Different channel types have different primary information needs. Tailor your support model accordingly.

| Channel Type | Primary Needs | Secondary Needs | Support Model |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Value-Added Resellers (VARs)** | Solution info, opportunity support | Competitive intelligence | High-touch, deal-focused |
| **Distributors** | Product updates, pricing, packaging | Market trends | Systematic, scalable |
| **System Integrators (SIs)** | Technical architecture, integration | Use cases, roadmap | Technical depth |
| **OEM Partners** | Technical specs, API/SDK docs | Roadmap, pricing | Engineering-focused |
| **Referral Partners** | Market intelligence, qualifying criteria | Solution overview | Light-touch, lead-focused |
| **Managed Service Providers (MSPs)** | Operational guides, support processes | Product updates | Operational depth |
| **Technology Alliance Partners** | Integration docs, joint use cases | Co-marketing materials | Partnership-focused |

## Information Tiering by Partner Level

If the organization uses partner tiers, information access and support levels should align.

### Tier 1 (Strategic / Platinum)
- Full access to all three information categories
- Dedicated channel manager
- Priority deal support with defined SLAs
- Early access to roadmap and pre-release information
- Co-marketing and co-selling resources
- Quarterly business reviews with market intelligence briefings

### Tier 2 (Growth / Gold)
- Full market intelligence and solution information
- Deal support through shared channel team
- Standard SLA on opportunity support requests
- Monthly update communications

### Tier 3 (Registered / Silver)
- Self-service access to market intelligence and solution information
- Deal support for qualified opportunities above threshold
- Quarterly update communications
- Partner portal access with knowledge base

## Gathering Partner Information Needs

Don't assume you know what partners need. Apply the Pragmatic principle of NIHITO (Nothing Important Happens In The Office) to your channel support.

**Methods for identifying partner information needs:**
- Partner advisory councils — structured feedback from top partners
- Post-deal debriefs — what information helped or was missing during deals
- Partner satisfaction surveys — quarterly pulse on information quality and gaps
- Channel manager field reports — patterns observed across partner interactions
- Support ticket analysis — what questions partners ask repeatedly (candidates for proactive content)
- Win/loss analysis filtered for channel deals — what information gaps contributed to losses
