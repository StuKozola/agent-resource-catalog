# Asset Audit Template

Use this template to assess the current state of the assets teams use to sell, support and explain the product. The audit identifies gaps across the selling process, the teams who use assets, and buyer personas, and helps prioritize which assets to build, update or retire. Parts 1–2 cover selling assets; Part 2b covers support and explanation assets.
