## Governance and Review

### Editorial Standards
- [ ] Brand voice and style guide documented and shared with all contributors
- [ ] Legal and compliance review process defined for regulated content
- [ ] Approval workflow documented (who signs off, in what order)
- [ ] Content tagging taxonomy defined (persona, stage, pillar, topic, format)

### Review Cadence

| Review | Frequency | Participants | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Content standup | Weekly | Content team | Production status, blockers, priorities |
| Editorial review | Bi-weekly | Content + product marketing | Quality, alignment, messaging consistency |
| Performance review | Monthly | Content + marketing ops | Metrics review, optimization actions |
| Strategic review | Quarterly | Content + marketing leadership + sales | Plan effectiveness, gap reassessment, next quarter planning |

### Plan Change Process
When priorities shift (new competitive threat, accelerated launch, executive request):

1. Assess impact on current calendar — what gets displaced?
2. Evaluate the new request against prioritization criteria (gap severity, program alignment, reuse potential, resource availability)
3. Get stakeholder agreement on trade-offs before committing
4. Update the calendar and communicate changes to all contributors
