# Positioning Document Examples

Two complete examples demonstrating the Pragmatic positioning template in action. The first is a B2B SaaS product; the second is a physical product. Both follow the document structure from `document-template.md`.

---

## Example 1: B2B SaaS — CloudLoxx (Cloud Security Platform)

### CloudLoxx — Positioning for IT Security Manager

**Version:** 2026-03-01
**Owner:** Sarah Chen, Product Marketing Manager
**Status:** Approved

---

#### 1. Target Buyer Persona

**Role:** IT Security Manager

**Context:** This buyer manages security across multiple cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP) for a mid-market company with 500–2,000 employees. They report to the CISO or VP of IT and are responsible for preventing breaches, maintaining compliance, and responding to incidents. Success means zero critical security incidents and clean audit results. They're stretched thin — typically managing 3–5 security tools that don't talk to each other.

**Role in Purchase Decision:** Lead Evaluator (Technical Buyer). Recommends to the CISO who holds budget authority.

---

#### 2. Problem Statement

**The Problem:**
Managing security across multiple cloud providers means juggling separate dashboards, inconsistent policies, and blind spots between environments. When an alert fires in AWS, there's no quick way to know if the same vulnerability exists in Azure. The security team spends more time switching between tools than actually remediating threats.

**Impact of the Problem:**
Security gaps between cloud environments are the leading cause of breaches in multi-cloud companies. The average breach costs $4.5M and takes 277 days to identify. Beyond the financial impact, a breach means months of remediation work and potential career consequences for the security leader who missed it.

---

#### 3. Ideal Solution

The ideal solution would provide a single view of security posture across all cloud environments, automatically detect misconfigurations and threats, and enforce consistent policies without requiring the team to become experts in each cloud provider's native security tools.

---

#### 4. Primary Message (25 Words or Less)

> CloudLoxx gives IT security teams one platform to see, protect, and automate security across every cloud environment — eliminating blind spots between providers.

**Word count:** 23

---

#### 5. Product Description

CloudLoxx consolidates multi-cloud security monitoring into a single platform, so security teams can detect threats and misconfigurations across AWS, Azure, and GCP without switching between tools. Automated compliance checks run continuously against common frameworks (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA), surfacing violations before auditors find them. When incidents occur, built-in remediation workflows reduce response time from hours to minutes.

---

#### 6. Key Capabilities

| Capability | Buyer Outcome |
|-----------|---------------|
| Unified multi-cloud dashboard | See your entire security posture in one place instead of switching between three provider consoles |
| Automated compliance monitoring | Stay audit-ready at all times — violations are flagged the moment they appear, not during the audit |
| Cross-cloud threat correlation | Detect attack patterns that span multiple environments, which single-cloud tools miss entirely |
| One-click remediation playbooks | Respond to incidents in minutes instead of hours by automating the most common remediation steps |

---

#### 7. Proof Points

- **Customer Result:** Meridian Financial reduced their mean time to detect cross-cloud threats from 14 hours to 22 minutes within 90 days of deployment.
- **Metric:** Customers report an average 67% reduction in time spent on compliance preparation.
- **Analyst Recognition:** Named a "Strong Performer" in the 2025 Forrester Wave for Cloud Security Posture Management.
- **Third-Party Validation:** SOC 2 Type II certified; FedRAMP Moderate authorized.

---

#### 8. Competitive Context

**Primary Alternative:** Most companies use each cloud provider's native security tools (AWS Security Hub, Azure Defender, Google Security Command Center) plus a SIEM for aggregation.

**Your Differentiation:** Native tools don't correlate across providers, and SIEMs require extensive configuration to normalize multi-cloud data. CloudLoxx provides cross-cloud correlation and automated remediation out of the box, with no custom integration work.

## Example 2: Physical Product — ProTrim 40 (Trailer-Mounted Aerial Lift)

### ProTrim 40 — Positioning for Small Business Owner / Operator

**Version:** 2026-02-15
**Owner:** Mike Reeves, Product Manager
**Status:** Approved

---

#### 1. Target Buyer Persona

**Role:** Small business owner operating a tree service, sign installation, or general contracting company with 2–15 employees.

**Context:** This buyer runs the business and works on job sites. They make purchasing decisions based on what will help complete more jobs per week and keep crews safe. They don't have a fleet manager or procurement department — they're comparing options on evenings and weekends. They value equipment that is simple, reliable, and doesn't require a dedicated mechanic.

**Role in Purchase Decision:** Decision Maker (Financial and Functional Buyer combined). They're spending their own money and using the equipment themselves.

---

#### 2. Problem Statement

**The Problem:**
Getting workers safely to 35–40 feet for tree trimming, sign work, or building maintenance requires either renting a boom lift every time (expensive and logistically painful) or buying a truck-mounted unit (which costs $80K+ and ties up a vehicle). Rental availability is unpredictable, and every day waiting for a rental is a day of lost revenue.

**Impact of the Problem:**
A small operator loses $1,200–$2,500 per day when they can't get a lift to the job site. Over a season, rental costs alone can exceed $15,000, and the scheduling uncertainty means turning down jobs that require aerial access.

---

#### 3. Ideal Solution

The ideal solution would be an aerial lift the operator owns, that any truck in the fleet can tow to a job site, that runs all day without engine maintenance, and that any crew member can operate after basic training.

---

#### 4. Primary Message (25 Words or Less)

> ProTrim 40 is a towable, all-electric aerial lift that reaches 40 feet — so you own your uptime instead of renting it.

**Word count:** 22

---

#### 5. Product Description

ProTrim 40 is a trailer-mounted boom lift that any pickup truck can tow to the job site. Because it runs on rechargeable batteries instead of a gas or diesel engine, there's no engine maintenance, no fuel costs, and no emissions — just plug it in overnight and it's ready for a full day of work. At 34 inches wide, it fits through most gates and man doors to reach tight work areas that truck-mounted lifts can't access.

---

#### 6. Key Capabilities

| Capability | Buyer Outcome |
|-----------|---------------|
| 40-foot working height, 19-foot horizontal reach | Handle the jobs that account for 80% of residential and commercial aerial work without renting a bigger unit |
| All-electric operation (no engine) | Eliminate fuel and engine maintenance costs — charge overnight from a standard outlet |
| 34-inch compact width | Access backyards, gated properties, and indoor spaces that truck-mounted units cannot reach |
| 500-pound platform capacity | Send two workers up with tools and materials in a single trip |
| Towable by any vehicle with a standard hitch | No dedicated truck required — any crew member can tow it to the job site |

---

#### 7. Proof Points

- **Customer Result:** Dave's Tree Service (6-person crew) recouped the purchase price in 5 months by eliminating rental costs and taking on 3 additional jobs per week.
- **Metric:** Average total cost of ownership over 5 years is 40% lower than renting equivalent equipment for the same number of job-days.
- **Third-Party Validation:** ANSI A92.2 compliant; meets all OSHA aerial lift safety requirements.

---

#### 8. Competitive Context

**Primary Alternative:** Renting a boom lift per job ($350–$600/day plus delivery) or purchasing a truck-mounted unit ($80K–$120K that permanently ties up a vehicle).

**Your Differentiation:** ProTrim 40 costs a fraction of a truck-mounted unit, doesn't require a dedicated vehicle, and eliminates the scheduling uncertainty of rentals. The all-electric design removes the engine maintenance that is the number-one reliability complaint with competitive trailer-mounted lifts.

---

## Key Observations Across Both Examples

1. **Problem first, product second.** In both documents, the reader encounters the problem before they hear about the product. By the time they reach the primary message, they already understand why they should care.

2. **Buyer language throughout.** The SaaS example talks about "switching between tools" and "blind spots" — not "multi-cloud abstraction layers." The physical product example talks about "lost revenue" and "towing to the job site" — not "z-boom articulation range."

3. **Primary messages are specific and differentiated.** Neither could have a competitor's name substituted in without becoming false. CloudLoxx's cross-cloud correlation is specific; ProTrim's towable all-electric design is specific.

4. **Capabilities tied to outcomes.** Every feature in the capabilities table has a "so what?" that matters to the specific buyer reading the document.

5. **Proof points are concrete.** Named customers, specific metrics, recognized certifications — not "many customers love our product."
