# Sales Alignment Document Template

Use this template to produce the final Sales Alignment deliverable. Adapt section depth based on available data and organizational maturity.

---

## Section 1: Executive Summary

Provide a concise overview (half-page to one page) covering:

- **Purpose:** Why this Sales Alignment initiative was undertaken
- **Scope:** Which product(s), market segment(s), and personas are covered
- **Key Findings:** The 3-5 most significant alignment gaps discovered
- **Top Recommendations:** The highest-priority actions to improve alignment
- **Expected Impact:** What improved alignment will deliver (faster deal velocity, higher win rates, reduced "no decision" losses, improved seller productivity)

### Review Cadence

- **Monthly:** Review leading indicators; adjust content priorities based on seller feedback

- **Annually:** Full Sales Alignment review; reassess buying process for market changes; realign selling process as needed
