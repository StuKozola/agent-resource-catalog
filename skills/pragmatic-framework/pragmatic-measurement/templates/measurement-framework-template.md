# Measurement Framework Template

## Instructions
Complete each section to create a measurement framework that connects your product, strategy and go-to-market work to corporate goals. This template implements the Measurement focus area of the 2027 Pragmatic Framework (Business category): *Which indicators tell us early whether this is working?* It sets the indicators, leading and lagging. Results for the organization are reviewed in Business Outcomes; whether customers got their outcome is Customer Value Realization.

## 8. Quarterly Tuning Review Checklist

Use this checklist at each quarterly review to ensure continuous improvement:

- [ ] All KPIs reviewed against targets — are targets still realistic and relevant?
- [ ] Corporate goals checked — have they shifted? Do KPIs need to change?
- [ ] Did the leading indicators predict the lagging results? Replace any that didn't
- [ ] Underperforming initiatives identified — root causes documented
- [ ] High-performing initiatives identified — can they be scaled?
- [ ] Budget reallocation recommendations prepared (shift from low to high performers)
- [ ] Attribution model producing reliable results? Any adjustments needed?
- [ ] New initiatives, programs or channels evaluated for inclusion in measurement framework
- [ ] Stakeholder feedback gathered — is reporting meeting their needs?
- [ ] Learnings documented for institutional knowledge
