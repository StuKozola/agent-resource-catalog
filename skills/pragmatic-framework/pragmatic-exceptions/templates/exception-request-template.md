## Decision criteria quick reference (for a policy document)

1. Does it fit the product strategy and target segment?
2. What is the underlying problem, and how many others have it?
3. What is the full cost, one-time and ongoing?
4. What value does it protect or create?
5. Can it be reversed, and what precedent does it set?
6. Is there a standard alternative?
7. Who is accountable for the consequence, and have they approved it?

Default positions: exceptions are time-boxed, owned and logged; anything that forks the product needs product leadership approval; anything requested by 3+ accounts goes to pattern review.
