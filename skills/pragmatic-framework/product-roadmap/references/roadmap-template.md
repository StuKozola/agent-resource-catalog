# Product Roadmap Templates

Use these templates as starting points. Adapt to your product, organization, and audience.

---

## Template 1: Theme-Based Roadmap (Now / Next / Later)

This is the recommended default format for most teams. It communicates priorities and direction
without over-committing to dates.

```markdown
# [Product Name] Product Roadmap
**Vision:** [1-2 sentence product vision]
**Last Updated:** [Date]
**Owner:** [Name/Role]

---

## NOW — Current Quarter ([Q_ 20__])
High confidence. Actively in progress or committed.

### Theme: [Theme Name]
**Problem:** [Market problem this addresses, in persona language]
**Personas:** [Target buyer/user personas]
**Success Metric:** [How we'll know this worked]
**Key Deliverables:**
- [Deliverable 1 — brief description]
- [Deliverable 2 — brief description]
**Dependencies:** [Cross-team or external dependencies]
**Status:** [On Track / At Risk / Blocked]

### Theme: [Theme Name]
[Same structure]

---

## NEXT — Following Quarter ([Q_ 20__])
Medium confidence. Planned but subject to adjustment based on learnings.

### Theme: [Theme Name]
**Problem:** [Market problem]
**Personas:** [Target personas]
**Success Metric:** [Target outcome]
**Key Initiatives:**
- [Initiative 1 — less specific than "deliverables" at this horizon]
- [Initiative 2]
**Open Questions:** [What we still need to learn before committing]

---

## LATER — 2+ Quarters Out
Low confidence. Directional. Will be refined as we learn more.

### Theme: [Theme Name]
**Problem:** [Market problem]
**Exploration Needed:** [What research or validation is required before this moves to Next]

---

## NOT DOING (This Cycle)
Explicitly deprioritized. Documented for transparency.

- [Theme/Request] — Reason: [Why it's not prioritized now]
- [Theme/Request] — Reason: [Why it's not prioritized now]

---

## Recently Completed
- [Theme] — Delivered [Date]. [Brief outcome/metric if available]
```

## Template 2: Quarterly Roadmap (Rolling 4-8 Quarters)

Better for organizations with structured planning cycles and executive audiences that expect
time-bound plans.

```markdown
# [Product Name] Quarterly Roadmap
**Vision:** [Product vision statement]
**Planning Horizon:** [Start Quarter] through [End Quarter]
**Last Updated:** [Date]

---

## [Q1 20__] — [Quarter Theme or Focus Area]
**Strategic Objective:** [What this quarter achieves toward the vision]

| Theme | Key Initiatives | Target Outcome | Confidence |
|-------|----------------|----------------|------------|
| [Theme A] | [Init 1, Init 2] | [Metric/Outcome] | Committed |
| [Theme B] | [Init 1, Init 2] | [Metric/Outcome] | Committed |

**Capacity Allocation:**
- [Theme A]: ~X% of engineering capacity
- [Theme B]: ~Y% of engineering capacity
- Tech debt / maintenance: ~Z%

---

## [Q2 20__] — [Quarter Theme or Focus Area]
**Strategic Objective:** [What this quarter achieves]

| Theme | Key Initiatives | Target Outcome | Confidence |
|-------|----------------|----------------|------------|
| [Theme C] | [Init 1, Init 2] | [Metric/Outcome] | Planned |
| [Theme A cont.] | [Init 3] | [Metric/Outcome] | Planned |

---

## [Q3-Q4 20__] — Directional
| Theme | Problem Being Solved | Confidence |
|-------|---------------------|------------|
| [Theme D] | [Market problem] | Exploratory |
| [Theme E] | [Market problem] | Exploratory |

---

## Key Dependencies and Risks
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|------|--------|------------|-------|
| [Risk 1] | [Impact] | [Plan] | [Who] |

---

## Assumptions
- [Key assumption 1 — e.g., headcount, market condition, partner timeline]
- [Key assumption 2]
```

## Template 3: Outcome-Based Roadmap

Best for product-led growth organizations using OKRs or similar goal frameworks.

```markdown
# [Product Name] Outcome-Based Roadmap
**Vision:** [Product vision]
**Planning Period:** [Timeframe]
**Last Updated:** [Date]

---

## Outcome 1: [Measurable Outcome Statement]
**Objective:** [What we're trying to achieve]
**Key Result:** [Specific metric and target]
**Why This Matters:** [Market evidence — what personas told us, win/loss data, etc.]

### Initiatives Contributing to This Outcome
| Initiative | Description | Status | Confidence |
|-----------|-------------|--------|------------|
| [Init A] | [Brief desc] | In Progress | High |
| [Init B] | [Brief desc] | Planned | Medium |

### How We'll Measure Success
- **Leading indicator:** [Early signal — e.g., engagement metric]
- **Lagging indicator:** [Outcome metric — e.g., retention rate]
- **Baseline:** [Current value]
- **Target:** [Goal value and timeframe]

---

## Outcome 2: [Measurable Outcome Statement]
[Same structure]

---

## Outcome 3: [Measurable Outcome Statement]
[Same structure]

---

## Capacity Allocation Across Outcomes
| Outcome | % of Capacity | Rationale |
|---------|:---:|-----------|
| Outcome 1 | X% | [Why this gets this allocation] |
| Outcome 2 | Y% | [Why] |
| Tech debt & reliability | Z% | [Why] |

---

## What We're NOT Pursuing This Period
| Potential Outcome | Reason for Deferral |
|------------------|-------------------|
| [Outcome X] | [Evidence-based reason] |
```

## Template 4: Product Marketing Roadmap

For product marketing teams building their own roadmap aligned with the technical product roadmap.
Based on the Pragmatic Framework's approach to go-to-market planning.

```markdown
# [Product Name] Product Marketing Roadmap
**Aligned to Technical Roadmap Version:** [Date]
**Last Updated:** [Date]

---

## [Quarter] — Go-to-Market Focus

### Theme: [Technical Roadmap Theme]
**Target Persona:** [Buyer persona]
**Positioning Angle:** [Key message / value prop]
**Competitive Implication:** [How this changes competitive dynamics]

**Marketing Deliverables:**
| Deliverable | Owner | Target Date | Status |
|------------|-------|-------------|--------|
| Positioning document | [Name] | [Date] | [Status] |
| Sales battlecard update | [Name] | [Date] | [Status] |
| Blog post / thought leadership | [Name] | [Date] | [Status] |
| Customer case study | [Name] | [Date] | [Status] |
| Webinar / event content | [Name] | [Date] | [Status] |
| Sales enablement training | [Name] | [Date] | [Status] |

**Launch Tier:** [Tier 1 (major) / Tier 2 (moderate) / Tier 3 (minor)]
**Win/Loss Insight Informing This:** [Summary of what the market told us]

---

[Repeat for each theme per quarter]

---

## Cross-Cutting Initiatives
Activities not tied to a specific product theme but essential to product marketing effectiveness.

| Initiative | Quarter | Owner | Notes |
|-----------|---------|-------|-------|
| Competitive SWOT refresh | [Q_] | [Name] | [Context] |
| Buyer persona update | [Q_] | [Name] | [Context] |
| Win/loss analysis cycle | [Q_] | [Name] | [Context] |
```

## Tips for Using These Templates

1. **Start with Template 1 (Now/Next/Later)** if you're building your first roadmap or if your
   organization is new to theme-based roadmapping. It's the simplest to maintain and hardest to
   misuse.

2. **Use Template 2 (Quarterly)** if your organization runs on a quarterly planning cadence and
   leadership expects time-bound commitments.

3. **Use Template 3 (Outcome-Based)** if your organization uses OKRs and wants tight alignment
   between product roadmap and company objectives.

4. **Use Template 4 (Product Marketing)** alongside any of the above when product marketing needs
   its own operational plan derived from the technical roadmap.

5. **Combine templates** as needed. Many teams use Template 1 for external communication and
   Template 2 for internal planning.

6. **Don't fill in every field on day one.** It's better to have a roadmap with some gaps that
   you iterate on than to delay sharing a roadmap because it's not "complete."
