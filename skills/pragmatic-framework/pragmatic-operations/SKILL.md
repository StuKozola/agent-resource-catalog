# Pragmatic Framework: Operations

## Overview

**Operations** is one of four activities in the **Support** category of the Pragmatic Framework (alongside Programs, Events, and Channels). The Support category sits on the execution side of the framework and represents the ongoing responsibility of product marketing to provide market and solution knowledge to the teams that execute go-to-market activities.

**Official Pragmatic Definition:**
> Provide needed market and solution information to support the operations group.

**What "the operations group" means:** The operations group encompasses the teams responsible for the systems, processes, data, and tools that power marketing and sales execution — marketing operations (MOps), sales operations (SOps), revenue operations (RevOps), and business operations. These teams manage CRM configuration, marketing automation platforms, lead scoring models, campaign infrastructure, data hygiene, reporting dashboards, and process workflows.

**Why this matters:** Operations teams build and maintain the infrastructure that turns strategy into execution. Without market context — buyer personas, segmentation data, buying process stages, competitive positioning — they configure systems in a vacuum. When product marketing actively supports operations with market and solution intelligence, the result is better-targeted automation, more accurate lead scoring, cleaner segmentation, and campaigns that reflect how the market actually buys.

## Required Skills (from Pragmatic Roles & Responsibilities)

- Deep understanding of the market and market problems
- Relationship building
- Business and technical acumen
- Written and verbal communications

## Workflow

When a user needs help with the Operations activity, follow these six steps:

### Step 1: Assess the Operations Landscape
Help the user identify which operations groups they need to support and what information gaps exist.

**Key questions to explore:**
- Which operations teams exist (MOps, SOps, RevOps, BizOps)?
- What systems and platforms do they manage (CRM, MAP, CDP, BI tools)?
- What market and solution information do they currently receive?
- Where are the gaps between what ops teams need and what product marketing provides?

> **Load reference:** For detailed guidance on conducting an operations needs assessment, read `references/operations-needs-assessment.md`.

### Step 2: Map Information Requirements
Help the user document what specific market and solution information each operations function needs.

**Core information categories:**
- **Buyer & segment data** — persona definitions, firmographic/demographic attributes, segment criteria for list building and targeting
- **Buying process stages** — how stages map to lifecycle/pipeline stages in CRM/MAP
- **Solution architecture** — product capabilities, packaging, SKUs, and how they map to market problems
- **Competitive context** — competitive flags, displacement triggers, and battle card data for routing/scoring
- **Messaging frameworks** — positioning by segment and persona for email templates, nurture tracks, and campaign themes

### Step 3: Create Operations Support Deliverables
Guide the user in producing the artifacts operations teams need.

**Common deliverables include:**
- Operations support brief (the primary artifact)
- Persona-to-system mapping documents
- Lead scoring criteria informed by buyer personas and buying stages
- Segment definitions for CRM/MAP list configuration
- Campaign theme briefs with market context for nurture workflows
- Solution-to-SKU mapping for quoting and CPQ systems

> **Load reference:** For the operations support brief structure and best practices, read `references/operations-support-brief.md`.

### Step 4: Align with Systems and Processes
Help the user translate market knowledge into actionable system requirements.

**Translation areas:**
- Persona attributes → CRM fields and picklist values
- Buying stages → lifecycle stage definitions and transition triggers
- Market segments → list segmentation rules and dynamic lists
- Competitive intelligence → lead routing rules and alert triggers
- Solution packaging → product catalog and CPQ configuration
- Campaign themes → nurture track design and content mapping

### Step 5: Establish Ongoing Support Cadence
Help the user build a sustainable rhythm for keeping operations teams informed.

**Cadence elements:**
- Regular sync meetings (monthly or quarterly) with ops leadership
- Market intelligence updates when positioning, personas, or segments change
- Launch coordination checklists that include ops requirements
- Feedback loops so ops teams can surface data quality issues or market signal gaps

### Step 6: Measure and Improve
Help the user define how to assess whether operations support is effective.

**Indicators of effective ops support:**
- Lead scoring accuracy (MQL-to-SQL conversion rates)
- Segmentation quality (campaign engagement by segment)
- Data completeness (key persona fields populated in CRM)
- Speed of operational readiness for launches and campaigns
- Reduction in ad-hoc requests from ops teams

> **Load template:** For a ready-to-customize operations support brief, use `templates/operations-support-brief-template.md`.

## Relationship to Other Framework Activities

Operations support draws on and connects to many other Pragmatic Framework activities:

- **Buyer Personas / User Personas** → Persona definitions become CRM fields and segmentation criteria
- **Buyer Experience** → Buying stages map to lifecycle and pipeline stages
- **Positioning** → Messaging frameworks inform email templates and nurture content
- **Market Definition** → Segment definitions drive list building and targeting rules
- **Competitive Landscape** → Competitive intelligence feeds routing rules and alerts
- **Launch** → Ops teams need advance notice and system configuration for launches
- **Measurement** → Ops teams produce the data that measurement depends on
- **Programs** → Campaign execution depends on ops infrastructure informed by market knowledge
- **Sales Alignment** → Sales process stages align with ops pipeline configuration
