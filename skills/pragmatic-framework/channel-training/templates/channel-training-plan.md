## 4. Curriculum Outline

### Foundation Track (Required for All Channel Reps)

| Module | Topic | Duration | Format | Key Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Market Problems We Solve | [45 min] | [e-learning + live Q&A] | [Top problems by persona, urgency/pervasiveness, why buyers care] |
| F2 | Know Your Buyers | [30 min] | [e-learning] | [Persona profiles, motivations, language, role in buying decision] |
| F3 | The Buyer's Journey | [45 min] | [live virtual] | [Buying stages, information needs, barriers, aligned sales tools] |
| F4 | Our Positioning | [30 min] | [e-learning + practice] | [Positioning by persona, messaging framework, language to use/avoid] |
| F5 | Competitive Landscape | [45 min] | [live virtual + battle cards] | [Top alternatives, differentiation, objection handling, when to fight/walk] |
| F6 | Your Sales Toolkit | [30 min] | [e-learning + demo] | [Available tools, when to use each, how to deploy in selling conversations] |

### Advanced Track (For Experienced Reps and Certification)

| Module | Topic | Duration | Format | Key Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Multi-Persona Selling | [60 min] | [live virtual + role-play] | [Navigating deals with multiple buyers, aligning messaging] |
| A2 | Enterprise Selling Motion | [60 min] | [live virtual] | [Longer cycles, procurement, complex evaluation, champions] |
| A3 | Expansion and Cross-Sell | [30 min] | [e-learning] | [Problem-based discovery in existing accounts, growth triggers] |
| A4 | [Launch-Specific Module] | [30 min] | [virtual + self-paced] | [New product selling guide, updated positioning, competitive changes] |

## 5. Delivery Plan

### Delivery Methods

| Method | Used For | Frequency |
|---|---|---|

| Live virtual sessions | Competitive updates, role-play, Q&A | Monthly or quarterly |
| In-person training | Annual partner kickoff, key partner onboarding | Annually + as needed |
| Microlearning | Weekly reinforcement, competitive tips | Weekly |
| Certification assessments | Competency validation | At end of each track |

| Phase | Activities | Timeline |
|---|---|---|

## 8. Measurement Plan

### Metrics by Tier

**Tier 1 — Activity (are they consuming training?):**
- [ ] Completion rates tracked by module and segment
- [ ] Certification rates tracked with time-to-certification
- [ ] Live session attendance rates

**Tier 2 — Behavior (are they selling differently?):**
- [ ] Call/demo review scores for positioning language adoption
- [ ] Sales tool utilization rates
- [ ] Discovery question quality assessments
- [ ] Rep confidence self-assessments (pre/post)

**Tier 3 — Outcomes (are results improving?):**
- [ ] Win rate by channel segment (pre vs. post)
- [ ] Time-to-first-deal for new partners
- [ ] Average deal size for channel-sourced opportunities
- [ ] Competitive win rate
- [ ] Partner-sourced revenue growth

### Reporting Cadence

| Report | Audience | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Training completion dashboard | Partner Management, Product Marketing | Weekly |
| Behavior change assessment | Product Marketing, Sales Leadership | Monthly |
| Business outcome correlation | Executive Leadership, Product Marketing | Quarterly |
