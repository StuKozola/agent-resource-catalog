## 2. Target Market & Positioning

- **Primary market segment(s)**: Who do they sell to? (industry, company size, geography)
- **Their stated positioning**: How do they describe themselves? (pull from their website/marketing — what problem do they claim to solve?)
- **Buyer personas they target**: (e.g., VP of Engineering, IT Director, End-user developer)
- **Market perception**: How do buyers and analysts actually describe them? (This often differs from their stated positioning.)

## 6. Winning Strategy Against This Competitor

### Ideal Matchup
Describe the deal profile where we are most likely to win against this competitor:
- Buyer persona most receptive to us
- Use cases where we excel
- Deal size / complexity sweet spot
- Industry or segment fit

### Deals to Avoid
Describe the deal profile where this competitor has a significant advantage:
- When the buyer prioritizes [specific capability]
- When [condition] is present
- When [persona] is the primary decision-maker

### Recommended Approach
- **Early-stage (before shortlist)**: How to position and what criteria to introduce
- **Mid-stage (active evaluation)**: Key proof points and demo emphasis
- **Late-stage (final selection)**: How to address concerns and close

### Key Messages
- Lead with: [1-2 sentences on our strongest positioning against this competitor]
- Acknowledge: [Where they're strong, and how to reframe]
