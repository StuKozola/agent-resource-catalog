## Scoring Guide

**Importance (1-3):** How critical is this capability to launch success?
- 3 = Critical — launch cannot succeed without this
- 2 = Important — significantly affects launch outcomes
- 1 = Nice to have — helpful but not essential

**Readiness (0-3):** How prepared is this area today?
- 3 = Fully ready — no action needed
- 2 = Mostly ready — minor gaps, easily addressed
- 1 = Significant gaps — requires meaningful effort to close
- 0 = Not ready — major work required

**Gap = Importance - Readiness** (positive values indicate action needed; higher values = higher priority)
