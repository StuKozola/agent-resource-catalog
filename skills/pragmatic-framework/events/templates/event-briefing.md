## Lead Qualification Criteria

### How to Qualify Event Contacts

For each interaction, assess and note:

1. **Persona fit:** Does this person match [Persona A] or [Persona B]?
2. **Problem acknowledged:** Did they describe experiencing [market problem]?
3. **Timeline:** Are they actively evaluating (within [timeframe])? Or researching?
4. **Authority:** Can they make or influence a purchase decision?
5. **Next step agreed:** Did they agree to a specific follow-up action?

### Lead Tiers

| Tier | Criteria | Follow-Up |
|------|----------|-----------|
| **Tier 1 (Hot)** | Matches target persona + active problem + near-term timeline | Sales follow-up within 48 hours |
| **Tier 2 (Warm)** | Persona fit + acknowledged problem + no immediate timeline | Marketing follow-up within 1 week |
| **Tier 3 (Nurture)** | General interest; some persona fit | Add to nurture program |

### Information to Capture

For every meaningful conversation, record:
- [ ] Name, title, company (badge scan + notes)
- [ ] Which persona they match
- [ ] The specific problem or use case they described
- [ ] Their current approach and pain points
- [ ] Timeline and buying stage
- [ ] Competitors they mentioned
- [ ] Agreed next step
- [ ] Lead tier (Hot / Warm / Nurture)
