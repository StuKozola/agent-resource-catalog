### Topics to Redirect

If asked about these topics, redirect gracefully:

- **Unreleased features/roadmap:** "We're focused on [current capabilities and the problems they solve]. I'd be happy to discuss our product direction in a 1:1 conversation."
- **Specific pricing:** "Pricing depends on your specific requirements. Let me connect you with our team to discuss your needs."
- **Internal company matters:** "I'm here to discuss [topic]. For company-specific questions, I'd point you to [appropriate channel]."

### Handling Difficult Questions

- **If you don't know the answer:** "That's a great question. I want to make sure I give you an accurate answer — can I follow up with you after the session?"
- **If the question is hostile:** Acknowledge the concern, reframe positively, bridge to your message
- **If the question is off-topic:** "Interesting question — that's a bit outside what we're covering today, but I'd love to discuss it after the session."

## Presentation Structure Guidance

### Recommended Flow (adjust for session length)

1. **Hook (first 2 minutes):** Open with a surprising statistic, a provocative question, or a relatable market challenge. Do NOT open with your bio, company overview, or agenda slide.

2. **Problem framing (20% of time):** Establish the market problem clearly. Use data and stories to make the audience feel the problem's urgency.

3. **Insight or framework (40% of time):** Deliver your core content — the insight, methodology, or framework that addresses the problem. This is the value the audience came for.

4. **Evidence (25% of time):** Support your insight with customer stories, data, and real-world examples.

5. **Takeaway and call to action (10% of time):** Summarize the one actionable thing the audience should do differently. Provide a clear next step.

6. **Q&A (remaining time):** Engage with audience questions using the preparation above.

### Slide Guidance

- Aim for 1 slide per 2-3 minutes of presentation time
- Prioritize visuals (charts, diagrams, photos) over text-heavy slides
- No more than 6 words per bullet; no more than 3 bullets per slide
- Include the company logo on the title and closing slides; subtle on content slides
- End with a clear call-to-action slide, not a "Thank You" or "Questions?" slide
