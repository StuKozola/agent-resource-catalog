# Pragmatic Framework — Programs Category

The **Programs** category is the execution engine of the Pragmatic Framework. While the left side of the framework (Market, Focus, Business, Planning) defines strategy, Programs translates that strategy into market-facing actions that generate awareness, move prospects through the funnel, acquire and retain customers, and measure results.

Programs contains **eight activities**: Marketing Plan, Revenue Growth, Revenue Retention, Launch, Awareness, Nurturing, Advocacy, and Measurement. Together they form a complete go-to-market execution system grounded in outside-in market knowledge.

## Pragmatic Principles That Govern Programs

Every program must trace back to market knowledge. The Pragmatic approach demands:

- **Outside-in thinking** — Programs are built on market problems, buyer personas, and buyer experience data, not on internal assumptions or product features
- **NIHITO (Nothing Important Happens In The Office)** — Program strategies should be validated with real market input, not designed in a conference room
- **Positioning as foundation** — Every program traces messaging back to positioning documents that describe the product by its ability to solve market problems
- **Measure outcomes, not activities** — Count pipeline created, not emails sent; count wins influenced, not assets produced

## Activity Definitions (Official Pragmatic Institute)

| Activity | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Marketing Plan** | Articulate the strategies and tactics for generating awareness and leads for the upcoming fiscal period, including key programs and events with measurements and goals |
| **Revenue Growth** | Define the specific plans and budgets for selling products and services to new customers |
| **Revenue Retention** | Define the specific plans and budgets for ensuring customer loyalty, as well as selling products and services to existing customers |
| **Launch** | Plan, execute and measure effectiveness of strategic launches |
| **Awareness** | Develop programs to raise the profile and awareness of your brand within strategic market segments to bring more prospects into your funnel |
| **Nurturing** | Develop programs to move prospects quickly and effectively through the funnel, with the objective of turning prospects into satisfied customers |
| **Advocacy** | Identify customers who are willing to give testimonials, case studies and references and amplify their voice in the market |
| **Measurement** | Measure and tune product marketing programs to ensure alignment with corporate goals |

## How Programs Connects to the Framework

### Upstream Inputs (Strategy → Programs)
- **Positioning** (Planning) → Foundation for all program messaging
- **Buyer Personas** (Planning) → Who each program targets
- **Buyer Experience** (Planning) → Where in the journey each program engages buyers
- **Competitive Landscape** (Market) → Differentiation messaging and competitive programs
- **Market Definition** (Focus) → Segment targeting for program investment
- **Business Plan** (Business) → Budget constraints and revenue expectations
- **Product Roadmap** (Focus) → Release timing drives launch and campaign sequencing

### Downstream Outputs (Programs → Enablement & Support)
- **Sales Alignment** (Enablement) → Programs inform how sales aligns selling to buying
- **Content** (Enablement) → Program needs drive content strategy and production
- **Sales Tools** (Enablement) → Programs generate demand that sales tools help convert
- **Channel Training** (Enablement) → Channel partners need program context to sell effectively

## Workflow for Each Activity

Each activity follows a six-step workflow. Load the appropriate reference file for detailed guidance.

---

### Marketing Plan
**Read:** `references/programs-guide.md` — Section: Marketing Plan

**Workflow:**
1. **Audit Inputs** — Confirm positioning, personas, buyer experience, and competitive landscape are current
2. **Set Strategic Goals** — Align program goals with business plan targets (revenue, pipeline, market share)
3. **Map Programs to Buyer Journey** — Build a persona × journey-stage matrix showing which programs reach which buyers at which stage
4. **Allocate Budget** — Distribute budget across programs based on expected impact, not historical precedent
5. **Define Calendar and Milestones** — Sequence programs across the fiscal period with clear milestones
6. **Establish Measurement Framework** — Define KPIs and targets for each program before execution begins

---

### Revenue Growth
**Read:** `references/programs-guide.md` — Section: Revenue Growth

**Workflow:**
1. **Define Target Segments and Personas** — Identify which market segments and buyer personas represent the best new-customer opportunities
2. **Set Acquisition Targets** — Establish revenue targets, new logo counts, pipeline coverage ratios, and CAC thresholds
3. **Design the Acquisition Engine** — Map the complete prospect-to-customer journey including awareness, nurturing, and sales handoff programs
4. **Build the Pipeline Model** — Create a funnel math model showing required inputs (leads, MQLs, SQLs, opportunities) to hit revenue targets
5. **Align with Sales** — Ensure marketing-sourced and marketing-influenced pipeline definitions are agreed upon with sales leadership
6. **Plan Budget and Resources** — Allocate spend across channels and programs based on expected CAC and pipeline contribution

---

### Revenue Retention
**Read:** `references/programs-guide.md` — Section: Revenue Retention

**Workflow:**
1. **Assess Current Retention Health** — Analyze NRR, GRR, churn rate, expansion revenue, and customer health scores
2. **Segment the Customer Base** — Identify at-risk, stable, and growth-opportunity segments within existing customers
3. **Design Retention Programs** — Build programs targeting onboarding success, adoption deepening, and churn prevention
4. **Design Expansion Programs** — Create upsell, cross-sell, and upgrade programs based on customer usage data and unmet needs
5. **Establish Customer Health Monitoring** — Define leading indicators of churn risk and expansion opportunity
6. **Set Targets and Budget** — Establish NRR targets, expansion revenue goals, and renewal rate objectives with allocated budget

---

### Launch
**Read:** `references/programs-guide.md` — Section: Launch

**Workflow:**
1. **Classify Launch Tier** — Determine launch scale (major, minor, maintenance) based on market impact
2. **Define Launch Goals** — Establish specific, measurable launch objectives tied to business outcomes
3. **Build Cross-Functional Plan** — Coordinate marketing, sales, product, support, and channel activities
4. **Prepare Enablement Materials** — Ensure sales tools, training, and customer-facing content are ready
5. **Execute Launch Sequence** — Run pre-launch, launch-day, and post-launch activities per the plan
6. **Measure and Report** — Evaluate launch effectiveness against goals and capture lessons learned

---

### Awareness
**Read:** `references/programs-guide.md` — Section: Awareness

**Workflow:**
1. **Define Awareness Goals** — Specify what awareness means for your market (brand recognition, thought leadership, share of voice)
2. **Identify Target Audiences** — Determine which personas and segments need awareness investment
3. **Select Channels and Tactics** — Choose channels based on where target personas consume information (not where you prefer to publish)
4. **Develop Thought Leadership Strategy** — Create a content and speaking strategy that demonstrates expertise on market problems
5. **Build the Program Calendar** — Schedule campaigns, content, events, and speaking engagements across the fiscal period
6. **Define Awareness Metrics** — Establish measurable KPIs (reach in target segments, share of voice, website traffic from target personas, aided/unaided recall)

---

### Nurturing
**Read:** `references/programs-guide.md` — Section: Nurturing

**Workflow:**
1. **Map the Nurturing Journey** — Define how prospects move from awareness through evaluation to purchase, aligned to the buyer experience
2. **Define Lead Stages and Scoring** — Establish MQL/SQL definitions and lead scoring criteria collaboratively with sales
3. **Design Nurturing Sequences** — Build content-driven nurturing paths for each persona at each funnel stage
4. **Implement Automation and Triggers** — Set up marketing automation workflows tied to prospect behavior and engagement signals
5. **Establish Sales Handoff Process** — Define when and how marketing-qualified leads are handed to sales, with clear SLAs
6. **Measure Funnel Performance** — Track conversion rates between stages, velocity, and lead quality to tune programs continuously

---

### Advocacy
**Read:** `references/programs-guide.md` — Section: Advocacy

**Workflow:**
1. **Identify Potential Advocates** — Find satisfied customers with measurable success stories who are willing to participate
2. **Design the Advocacy Program** — Create a structured program with clear participation options (testimonials, case studies, references, speaking, reviews)
3. **Capture Customer Stories** — Develop case studies and testimonials that connect customer outcomes to market problems your product solves
4. **Build the Reference Database** — Create a searchable, segmented database of advocates organized by industry, use case, persona, and participation type
5. **Activate Advocates Across Programs** — Deploy customer stories in awareness, nurturing, sales, and launch programs
6. **Maintain and Grow the Program** — Recognize advocates, prevent advocate fatigue, and continuously recruit new participants

---

### Measurement
**Read:** `references/programs-guide.md` — Section: Measurement

**Workflow:**
1. **Clarify Corporate Goals** — Understand what the business is trying to achieve so metrics connect to outcomes
2. **Map Programs to Outcome Metrics** — Connect each program to measurable business results (not just activity metrics)
3. **Select KPIs** — Choose metrics that are aligned, measurable, actionable, timely, and understandable
4. **Build the Measurement Framework** — Create a dashboard structure with leading and lagging indicators
5. **Establish the Tune Process** — Define review cadences and decision protocols for adjusting programs based on data
6. **Present to Stakeholders** — Report marketing impact in business language that connects programs to corporate goals
