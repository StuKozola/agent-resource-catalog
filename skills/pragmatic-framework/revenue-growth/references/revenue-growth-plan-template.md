# Revenue Growth Plan Template

Use this template to structure a comprehensive Revenue Growth Plan. Adapt sections based on the user's context — not every organization needs every section at the same depth.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Purpose**: One-page overview for leadership alignment.

- Planning period (e.g., FY2026, Q1-Q4)
- New-customer revenue target: $________
- New-logo count target: ________
- Total acquisition budget: $________
- Target CAC: $________
- Expected CLV:CAC ratio: ________
- Key strategic bets (2-3 sentence summary of primary acquisition strategies)

---

## 2. Market Context and Inputs

Summarize the upstream Pragmatic Framework inputs that inform this plan.

### 2.1 Target Segments

For each priority segment:
- Segment name and description
- Estimated segment size (number of potential customers)
- Alignment with SAM/SOM from Market Definition
- Segment priority ranking and rationale

### 2.2 Buyer Personas

For each target buyer persona:
- Persona name and role
- Key problems they are trying to solve
- How they discover and evaluate solutions
- Primary buying criteria
- Typical buying process and timeline

### 2.3 Competitive Context

- Primary competitors for new-customer attention
- Key competitive differentiators (from Distinctive Competencies)
- Competitive strategy summary (from Competitive Landscape)

### 2.4 Positioning Summary

- Core positioning statement for new-customer acquisition
- Segment-specific positioning variations (if applicable)

### 2.5 Pricing for New Customers

- Pricing model summary
- New-customer-specific pricing (introductory offers, trial pricing, land-and-expand)
- Average expected deal size for new customers

## 10. Review Cadence

- **Weekly**: Leading indicators (MQL/SQL volume, demo requests, pipeline activity)

- **Quarterly**: Full plan review — budget vs. actual, CAC analysis, channel ROI, plan adjustments
- **Annually**: Comprehensive review feeding into next fiscal year planning
