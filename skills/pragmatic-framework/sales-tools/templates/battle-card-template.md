## Landmine Questions

Questions to plant that expose competitor weaknesses without directly attacking. Let the prospect discover the gap on their own.

1. "Have you had a chance to ask [Competitor] about [area of weakness]?"
2. "What's their approach to [capability where you excel and they don't]?"
3. "How does [Competitor] handle [scenario that exposes a limitation]?"

## Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge their strengths, then redirect | Trash-talk the competitor |
| Use landmine questions to let the buyer discover gaps | Make claims you can't substantiate |
| Lead with your value, not their weaknesses | Read this card verbatim to a prospect |
| Tailor your approach to the persona you're engaging | Use the same talking points for every buyer |
| Update this card when you learn something new | Assume this card is complete and final |
