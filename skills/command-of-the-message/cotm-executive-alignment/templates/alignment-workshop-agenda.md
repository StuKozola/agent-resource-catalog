# Executive Alignment Workshop — Agenda Template

## Pre-work (due 1 week before)

- [ ] Buyer personas and use cases in scope
- [ ] Top market problems, with buyer quotes where available
- [ ] Competitive alternatives, including "do nothing"
- [ ] Win/loss findings from the last 2–4 quarters
- [ ] 3–5 customer success stories with quantified results
- [ ] 5–10 call recordings or emails showing current value messaging
- [ ] Each leader's written answers to the three essential questions:
  1. What problems do we solve?
  2. How do we solve them differently or better?
  3. Where have we done it before?

## Agenda (one day; split into two half-days if remote)

| Time | Session | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | **Sponsor opening:** business case, goals, what will be different this time | Shared purpose |
| 0:30–1:15 | **Where we are:** compare leaders' answers to the three questions; review call examples | Gap list |
| 1:15–2:15 | **Buyers and problems:** confirm personas and use cases; agree the top business issues and their negative consequences | Before scenarios, consequences |
| 2:15–2:30 | Break | |
| 2:30–3:30 | **Outcomes and metrics:** what the buyer gets and how they'll measure it | Positive business outcomes, metrics |
| 3:30–4:30 | **Required capabilities and differentiators:** what any solution needs; where we are unique, better, defensible | Capabilities, differentiators |
| 4:30–5:15 | **Proof:** match each differentiator to a proof point; flag gaps | Proof map, gap list |
| 5:15–5:45 | **Validation plan:** which customers and reps test the draft; owners and dates | Validation plan |
| 5:45–6:15 | **Commitments:** each leader states what their team will do differently and by when | Signed commitments |

## Facilitation tips

- Write in the buyer's words. Challenge any sentence that starts with the product name.
- Park feature debates; ask "what business outcome does this enable?"
- Apply the competitor swap test to differentiators: if a competitor could say it, it isn't one.
- Capture disagreements as open questions with owners rather than forcing consensus late in the day.

## Post-workshop (within 48 hours)

- [ ] Circulate the draft Value Framework and decisions with the sponsor's endorsement
- [ ] Confirm owners and dates for open questions
- [ ] Schedule validation interviews and rep reviews
