# Training and Certification — Command of the Message, Step 5

Training gives everyone the same understanding of the Value Framework; certification proves they can use it with a buyer. Both need to be role-specific, practice-heavy and repeatable for new hires.

**Prerequisites:** a validated Value Framework (`cotm-value-framework`) and field tools (`cotm-field-tools`). Managers should be trained before their teams so they can coach immediately afterward.

**Output:** a training plan by role, role-play scenarios, a certification rubric and schedule, and an onboarding path.

## Workflow

### 1. Define audiences and outcomes by role

| Role | They must be able to… |
|---|---|
| Account executives | Run discovery, deliver the mantra and persona talk tracks, use trap-setting questions, handle objections with value, build opportunity plans |
| SDRs / BDRs | Open with a before scenario and consequence, qualify interest against PBOs, hand off with notes mapped to the framework |
| Sales engineers | Demo against required capabilities and PBOs, not feature tours; quantify metrics |
| First-line managers | Coach to the framework in deal reviews and call reviews; certify their reps |
| Customer success / services | Use PBOs and metrics in onboarding, value reviews and renewals; surface proof points |
| Marketing / product marketing | Build campaigns, content and positioning from the framework |
| Product management | Check roadmap and launch messaging against outcomes and capabilities |
| Executives | Use the language in forecasts, all-hands, board and customer meetings |

### 2. Design the learning path

A typical sequence:

1. **Pre-work (self-paced, 1–2 hours):** methodology primer, the Value Framework, field tools.
2. **Live workshop (1–2 days, or spread over weeks):** concepts → framework walkthrough by persona → discovery practice → trap-setting practice → mantra practice → objections → applying it to the rep's own live deals.
3. **Application (2–4 weeks):** reps use the tools on real calls; managers review recordings.
4. **Certification:** scored role-plays (see step 4).
5. **Ongoing:** refreshers, new-hire cohorts, updates when the framework changes.

Practice should take at least half of live time. Use reps' own current opportunities wherever possible so training transfers directly.

### 3. Build role-play scenarios

Use `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/StuKozola/agent-resource-catalog/main/skills/command-of-the-message/cotm-training-certification/templates/role-play-scenario.md`. Each scenario defines a buyer persona, their hidden before state and consequences, what they care about, likely objections, and what a strong rep would uncover. Give the buyer role-player a separate brief so the rep has to discover information through questions.

### 4. Certify

Use `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/StuKozola/agent-resource-catalog/main/skills/command-of-the-message/cotm-training-certification/templates/certification-rubric.md`. Common certification components:

- **Mantra delivery:** clear, in the buyer's terms, under a minute.
- **Three essential questions:** answered for an assigned persona.
- **Discovery role-play:** uncovers before scenario, consequences, PBOs and metrics; uses at least one trap-setting question.
- **Objection handling:** reconnects to value and proof.
- **Opportunity plan:** a real deal mapped to the framework (and to MEDDPICC if used).

Managers or trained certifiers score with the rubric. Set a pass mark and allow re-certification after coaching. Track certification rates by team as a leading indicator (`cotm-impact-measurement`).

### 5. Build internal capacity

Identify enablement staff and strong managers to become internal trainers and certifiers. Run calibration sessions so certifiers score consistently. If the company works with Force Management directly, its formal train-the-trainer certification is the route for official delivery.

### 6. Plan the launch moment

A sales kickoff is a natural launch: the sponsor opens with the business case, top reps demonstrate the message, and teams practice on live deals. Avoid launching at the SKO without follow-up; certification and coaching must come within weeks.

### 7. Onboard new hires

Fold the framework, field tools and certification into onboarding so new reps are certified within their first 30–60 days. Ramp time is a key metric for the program.

## Common mistakes

- Lecture-heavy sessions with little practice.
- Training reps before managers.
- Certification that tests recall rather than live application.
- No path for new hires, so the message decays with turnover.
- Training marketing and customer success as an afterthought.

## Using AI in this step

- Generate role-play scenarios and buyer briefs from the framework.
- Act as a buyer persona for unlimited rehearsal.
- Pre-score recorded certification attempts against the rubric for certifier review.

Humans make certification decisions. Tell participants when AI is used to review their recordings and keep recordings in approved tools.
