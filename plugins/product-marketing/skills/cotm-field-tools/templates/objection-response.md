## Response structure

1. **Acknowledge** — show you understood the concern.
2. **Clarify** — ask a question to find what's really behind it.
3. **Reconnect to value** — restate the negative consequence of not acting and the outcome they said matters.
4. **Prove** — share a relevant proof point.
5. **Confirm** — check whether the concern is resolved and agree a next step.

## Common objections to prepare

- "It's too expensive." → consequence cost vs. investment; metric-based return
- "Now isn't the right time." → cost of waiting
- "We'll build it ourselves." → required capabilities, time to value, opportunity cost
- "We're happy with what we have." → before scenario and consequences the buyer may not have quantified
- "Competitor X does the same." → trap-setting questions and differentiators with proof
