# Command of the Message × MEDDPICC Mapping

| MEDDPICC element | What it means | Value Framework source | Suggested CRM field(s) | Deal review question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M — Metrics** | Quantified measures of the value the buyer expects | Metrics, PBOs | Metric name, baseline, target, buyer-confirmed (Y/N) | "What will the buyer measure, what is it today, and what do they expect it to become?" |
| **E — Economic buyer** | The person with final authority over the money | Persona with the decisive PBOs | Economic buyer (contact), met (Y/N), PBO they care about | "Have we heard the economic buyer describe the outcome in their own words?" |
| **D — Decision criteria** | How the buyer will compare options | Required capabilities, differentiators, trap-setting questions | Criteria list; criteria we influenced (Y/N) | "Which of our differentiators are in their criteria, and how did they get there?" |
| **D — Decision process** | The steps and people involved in deciding | Discovery | Steps, dates, approvers | "What happens between now and a signature, and who is involved?" |
| **P — Paper process** | Legal, procurement and security steps to contract | Discovery | Steps, owners, lead times | "What could delay the contract after they say yes?" |
| **I — Identify pain** | The business problem driving the purchase | Before scenario, negative consequences | Before scenario; consequence value | "What does it cost them to do nothing, and who feels it?" |
| **C — Champion** | An insider with influence who sells for you | Mantra, proof points | Champion (contact), tested (Y/N), can state value (Y/N) | "Can our champion explain the value without us in the room?" |
| **C — Competition** | Alternatives, including do-nothing and build | Differentiators, proof | Competitors; status quo risk | "Why will they choose us over the alternative they'd otherwise pick, including doing nothing?" |

## Notes

- Some teams use MEDDICC (without paper process) or other variants; the same mapping applies.
- "Buyer-confirmed" flags matter more than free text. A rep's hypothesis about metrics is not a confirmed metric.
- Keep fields to what managers will actually review. Remove any field no one has looked at after a quarter.

## Example value-based stage criteria

| Stage | Must be true to exit |
|---|---|
| Qualify | Pain identified; before scenario and consequences heard from the buyer |
| Discover | PBO and at least one metric confirmed; economic buyer identified |
| Validate | Decision criteria include at least one differentiator; champion identified and tested |
| Business case | Economic buyer accepts the quantified value; decision process mapped |
| Negotiate | Paper process mapped; mutual action plan agreed |
