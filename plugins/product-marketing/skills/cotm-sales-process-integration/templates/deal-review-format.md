# Value-First Deal Review Format

**Time per deal:** 10–15 minutes for strategic deals; 3–5 minutes in forecast calls.

## Order of questions

Ask value questions before amounts and dates.

1. **Pain and before state:** What's happening in the buyer's business today? Who told us?
2. **Consequences:** What does it cost them to do nothing? Has the buyer quantified it?
3. **Outcomes and metrics:** What outcome have they committed to, and how will they measure it?
4. **Economic buyer:** Have we heard the economic buyer describe that outcome?
5. **Decision criteria and differentiation:** Which of our differentiators are in their criteria? What proof have we shared?
6. **Champion:** Can our champion tell the story without us?
7. **Process:** What are the remaining decision and paper process steps?
8. **Only now:** amount, close date, forecast category.

## Forecast rules (examples)

- **Commit:** PBO and metric confirmed by the economic buyer; champion tested; paper process mapped.
- **Best case:** PBO confirmed by a stakeholder; economic buyer identified but not yet engaged.
- **Pipeline:** Pain identified; outcomes and metrics still hypotheses.

## Manager's coaching close

End each review with one specific action that strengthens the value story (e.g. "Get the CFO to confirm the baseline before Friday"). Record it in the opportunity plan.
